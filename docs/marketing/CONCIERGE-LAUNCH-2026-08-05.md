# BroLab concierge onboarding — launch brief

## Promise

Publish your first beat or audio service today. In a free 15-minute assisted setup, BroLab helps configure the storefront, connect Stripe and publish one sell-ready offer.

This is a hands-on activation service for the early-access cohort, not a generic demo and not an unlimited implementation service.

## Pilot cohort

- Wave 1: 10 high-intent X prospects — 5 producers and 5 engineers.
- Wave 2: next 20 prospects only after reviewing replies and booking friction.
- Remaining 70 prospects stay in reserve; revalidate their latest public signal before contact.

The import-ready pilot is in `CONCIERGE-WAVE-1-2026-08-05.txt`.

## Conversation sequence

1. Send the research-led opener. Do not include a product link.
2. If no reply, send one follow-up after 3–4 days.
3. After a relevant reply, explain the one-offer workflow.
4. Invite only qualified prospects to the free setup page with their attributed campaign URL.
5. Stop after a negative reply or a second unanswered message.

Every outbound message remains human-approved. No bulk auto-DM.

## Concierge session

Before the call, ask the prospect to have ready:

- one beat file or one clearly scoped service;
- current price and basic terms;
- access to their own Stripe account.

During the 15 minutes:

1. Confirm storefront name and URL.
2. Start the one-month BASIC or PRO trial.
3. Let the prospect connect Stripe themselves; never request credentials.
4. Publish one beat or one service.
5. Copy the public storefront link and agree on the next action.

If Stripe verification or file preparation blocks publication, finish the configuration and schedule one short asynchronous follow-up. The success criterion remains `first_offer_published`.

## Pilot metrics

- 10 researched openers sent
- at least 3 replies
- at least 2 qualified conversations
- at least 1 booked concierge setup
- at least 1 first offer published
- median time from signup to first offer under 24 hours

Scale the next wave only after the first session reveals where the product or call script creates friction.

## 📊 Messaging Guidelines

**Key Value Props (prioritize these):**
1. **0% commission** = Keep 100% of sales
2. **Own your brand** = Custom storefront, not lost in a marketplace
3. **Instant payouts** = Stripe direct (no waiting 30+ days)
4. **Professional** = License generation, analytics, mobile-optimized

**Objection Handling:**
- "I need traffic" → Most sales come from YOUR marketing (YouTube, IG, TikTok). You're paying 30% for hosting.
- "How do you make money?" → Small monthly subscription instead of commission.
- "I don't trust new platforms" → Fair. Here's a live demo + I'll personally onboard you.

**Tone:**
- Authentic, not salesy
- Producer-to-producer (we understand the struggle)
- Transparent about being new
- Confident but humble

**CTA Strategy:**
- Always offer demo link first (low friction)
- Offer personal setup help (high touch)
- Use FOMO sparingly (lifetime PRO for first 50)

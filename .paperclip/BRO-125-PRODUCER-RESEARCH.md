## Research Approach for Identifying Targets

### Tier 1: High-Quality Targets (10-50k followers)
**Profile**: Actively selling beats, have audience, engaged with platform
- Search: "#BeatSeller", "#BeatMaker selling beats"
- Look for: Link to Beatstars, Gumroad, or personal site in bio
- Signal: Recent posts about beat sales, pricing discussions

### Tier 2: Engaged Mid-Tier (1k-10k followers)
**Profile**: Regular content, active community participation, monetization-focused
- Search: "Just sold a beat", "beat sale", "producer life"
- Look for: Engagement metrics, regular posting cadence
- Signal: Comments on other producers' posts, questions about sales platforms

### Tier 3: Emerging Producers (Follow growth potential)
**Profile**: High engagement, growing, motivated by money
- Search: New accounts in production hashtags
- Look for: Fresh energy, asking monetization questions
- Signal: Rapid growth, high engagement %

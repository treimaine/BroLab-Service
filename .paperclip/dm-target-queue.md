## 🔍 How to Execute DM Queue (Launch Day)

### Step 1: Find Targets (15 minutes)

**X/Twitter Search:**
1. Go to https://twitter.com/search
2. Search: `"beats for sale" OR "new beat pack" min_faves:10`
3. Filter: Last 7 days, English, from people with 1K-10K followers
4. Open 20 promising profiles in new tabs

**Reddit Search:**
1. r/makinghiphop - search "feedback thread"
2. Find producers posting beats for critique
3. Check their post history for selling activity

**Instagram Search:**
1. Hashtag: #beatsforsale #typebeat #producerlife
2. Filter by accounts with 1K-10K followers
3. Check if they have link in bio (actively selling)

### Step 2: Research Each Target (2 min per producer)

**Quick Profile Scan:**
- ✅ Do they sell beats? (Beatstars link, "DM for beats", etc.)
- ✅ Is their content quality? (professional audio, good engagement)
- ✅ Are they active? (posted in last 7 days)
- ✅ Do they engage? (replies to comments, not just posting)

**Personalization Data:**
- Note their latest beat/project name
- Note one specific thing about their sound (808s, melody, vibe)
- Note any pain points mentioned

### Step 3: Send Personalized DMs (1-2 min per DM)

**DM Structure:**
1. **Opener:** Compliment + specific reference to their work
2. **Question:** "Are you selling beats right now?"
3. **Value Prop:** BroLab = keep 100% of sales
4. **CTA:** "Want me to send you the demo?"

**Timing:**
- Send 10 DMs immediately after X thread posts
- Send remaining 10 DMs over next hour
- Don't spam - space out by 5-10 minutes

### Step 4: Track Responses (Ongoing)

**Response Categories:**
- ✅ **Interested** → Send demo link immediately
- ❓ **Question** → Answer within 5 minutes
- 👍 **Positive but vague** → Follow up: "Can I send you the demo link?"
- ❌ **Not interested** → Thank them and move on

```

### "How is this different from Beatstars?"

```

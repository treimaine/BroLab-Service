## Quick Tips for Success

1. **Timing**: Post at 9 AM, 2 PM, 6 PM ET for max reach
2. **Authenticity**: Write like a real person, not a bot
3. **Responses**: Reply within 2 hours to comments (builds algorithm favor)
4. **Variety**: Mix problem/solution, demo, and community posts
5. **Hooks**: Start every post with a question or bold statement
6. **Links**: Use short, clean links (or bit.ly)
7. **Hashtags**: Use 3-4 relevant ones per post (#beatmakers, #makinghiphop, etc.)
8. **Visuals**: Add graphics/videos when available (increases engagement 3-5x)

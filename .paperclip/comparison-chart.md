## 🎯 Key Insights

### When BroLab Makes Sense:
✅ **You have your own audience** (YouTube, IG, TikTok, email list)
✅ **You're serious about selling beats** (not just browsing marketplace)
✅ **You want to own your customer relationships**
✅ **You make $100+/month in sales** (commission savings > subscription cost)

### When Beatstars/Airbit Makes Sense:
✅ **You're brand new** (need marketplace discovery)
✅ **You have zero audience** (relying 100% on marketplace traffic)
✅ **You make <$100/month** (marketplace exposure > commission cost)

### The Hybrid Approach:
💡 **Use both!**
- Keep Beatstars/Airbit for marketplace discovery
- Send your own audience (YouTube, IG, TikTok) to BroLab
- Keep 100% of sales from your audience
- Pay 20-30% only on marketplace-sourced sales
